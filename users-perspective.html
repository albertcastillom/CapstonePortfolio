User's Perspective

What you can do

If you are a person who gets distracted easily by there mobile device, you can use an accountability-based screen time app. This transfers the control of blocking apps to a person you trust. Before each productivity session you can include a to-do list, a note of your goals, or a timer. This keeps your friend in the loop and makes sure they can follow up or keep you accountable. The built in pomodoro also allows you to take breaks and focus on your tasks in a more efficient way. This timer would be able to unblock you apps after 50 minutes so you can take a 10 minute break, then reinitiate the block. Once your tasks are done you can have your friend unblock your apps early, if they approve.

Why it matters

This app is perfect for people who have an easier time following through on their goals when they have an accountability partner. This app is also great for people who want to be more productive, spend less time on their phone, or just want to be more focused. The app can be used for anything you want to be more focused on, whether it is studying, working, spending less time on a screen, or anything else it might be.

How it works

This screen time app works by allowing a user to send a friend a focus session request for approval. A focus session includes a duration, apps getting blocked, and an optional to-do list and notes. A friend can then approve the session which then starts the screen time block on your phone until the duration is over or you finish your tasks. If you finish the tasks before your timer then the same friend can unblock the apps early. In addition to this you have basic screen time features like a focus session that is initiated by yourself, a pomodoro timer block, and a focus session leaderboard for friends.

Key benefits

  • Accountability based focus session with someone trusted
  • Easier time getting tasks done with no distractions
  • Help friends also be more focused
  • Leaderboard to see who is the most focused for fun or to encourage competition
  • Spend less time on your phone
